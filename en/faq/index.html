Questions on barriers, floor tape and warehouse safety

The questions warehouse and EHS managers ask most when planning barriers, floor lines or a site survey. Each answer is short and links to the detail page.

Polymer barriers

Do polymer barriers need repainting?

No. The manufacturer's maintenance guide only covers cleaning, and checking bolts and joints on a schedule. See the polymer barrier maintenance schedule.

How heavy a forklift can a polymer barrier take?

Each barrier size has its own limit. We give a figure only when there is a manufacturer's test report, and the size is set when we measure on site. See the polymer barrier series.

Should we replace all our existing steel barriers?

No need. Keep steel on low-traffic lanes and switch to polymer on stretches that get hit constantly. See the steel or polymer article.

What stops forklifts reversing into the wall at a parking bay?

A wheel stop fixed to the floor at the end of the bay, plus a kick rail along the wall if needed. See the parking and charging area.

Floor tape

Can marking tape take forklifts running over it?

Yes, on stretches where vehicles run straight. Where wheels turn on the spot it wears faster and needs a look on site. See floor marking tape.

How do reflective and glow-in-the-dark tape differ?

Reflective tape only shines when a light hits it. Glow-in-the-dark tape glows by itself for a while after the lights go off. See reflective tape.

Does taping mean stopping the plant?

Not for long. Tape can be walked on right after it is laid, and you only need to fence off the stretch being taped. See how to apply tape so it sticks.

Is there a floor color chart to follow?

There is a chart based on 5S practice, but it is not a general mandatory rule. Each plant sets its own. See the factory floor marking color chart.

Forklifts and walkways

How wide should a walkway in a warehouse be?

There is no general figure for every warehouse. The width depends on how many people walk at once, whether trolleys use it and the plant's rules. See separating walkways and forklifts.

Where do impacts most often happen in a warehouse?

Rack ends, corners, columns beside lanes, dock doors and points where people cross the forklift lane. See the 7 zones to review.

Can a convex mirror replace a barrier at a corner?

No. A mirror helps the driver see people sooner, but it does not stop a vehicle. A corner where people pass still needs a barrier or a gate. See the three levels of separating people from forklifts.

What should we do first after a forklift clips a rack upright?

Have the person in charge of the racking inspect the whole rack bay, and do not load more goods into that bay until it is checked. Only then think about a guard. See the rack end zone.

Surveys and quotations

Can I check the warehouse myself before calling?

Yes. Use the 10-point checklist, walk the warehouse during a shift and photograph the spots you ticked. See the warehouse self-check.

Do you support warehouses outside the north?

The survey area is Hanoi and the northern provinces. If your warehouse is farther away, send photos first and we will talk. See book a survey.

Does Thien Khoi apply tape at the plant?

Yes. Thien Khoi applies tape at your plant after checking the floor. See book a survey.

Why does the website not show prices?

Price depends on the length, type and size in each zone, so Thien Khoi quotes after the survey. See book a survey.
