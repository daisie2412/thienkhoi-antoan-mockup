Warehouse safety documents

When to use each document

These documents are meant to be used on the shop floor. Pick the ones you need, leave your name and contact details, and Thien Khoi sends the right version by email or WhatsApp.

Before a survey

Use these two when you want to look at the warehouse yourself before you call.

  • 10-point warehouse survey checklist: print it on A4, carry it on a walk through the warehouse during a shift and mark issues on the spot. Each mark is a spot worth photographing and sending. The on-screen version is in the selection guide.
  • Separating people from forklifts, 3 levels: floor lines, barriers along walkways, gates at crossing points. Use it in a meeting with the forklift team to agree which stretch needs which level.

When choosing products

A catalogue describes the product. It does not tell you which size your warehouse needs. Barrier size and tape type are confirmed after measuring on site.

  • Polymer barrier catalogue: barrier series, sizes, installation and the manufacturer's maintenance guide. Use it once you know which stretch needs a barrier and want to compare sizes and base types.
  • Floor safety tape catalogue: marking, anti-slip, reflective and photoluminescent tape, colors, widths and application. Use it to settle the tape type for each area.
  • Floor marking color chart: one page to post at the shop floor entrance, one color per meaning. It follows common 5S practice, and each plant sets its own chart. See how in the floor marking color chart article.

For internal approval

The Thien Khoi company profile covers how we work from survey to installation, the two product lines, the contact person and the legal entity details. Purchasing teams and management often ask for it when assessing a new vendor. The legal entity details are also on the About Thien Khoi page.

How to get the documents

Tick the documents you need on the list on this page and fill in your name, phone or WhatsApp number, and email if you want them by email. Thien Khoi sends the right version by email or WhatsApp.

Why there is no direct download: the catalogues cover many series and sizes. When we know which area you are working on, we point out the part to read first, so you do not have to go through the whole book. Your details are used only to send the documents and follow up on your request, as set out in the privacy policy.

After reading

A marked-up checklist and the photos taken with it are enough to start. Send them through the survey booking page, and Thien Khoi will review the photos, call you back and book a survey during a running shift.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about our documents

See all questions
Why do I need to leave my name and phone number to get the documents?

So we can send the version you need, through the channel you use. The catalogues cover many series and sizes, and knowing which area you are working on lets us point out the part to read first. Your details are used only to send the documents and follow up on your request, as set out in the privacy policy.

Can I get the documents on WhatsApp instead of email?

Yes. Put your WhatsApp number in the contact field and leave the email blank if you do not need it. The files open on a phone. To print the checklist for the whole team, move the file to a computer and print on A4. A quick on-screen version of the checklist is on this website, in the selection guide.

Can the 10-point checklist serve as our plant's safety inspection record?

It should not replace the inspection records your plant keeps under its own rules. The checklist only helps the warehouse team see which spots to photograph and which to fix first. Your plant can add a few of its points to its own inspection form. Companies should check current occupational safety regulations. See how to use the checklist.

What is in the Thien Khoi company profile?

How we work from survey to installation, the two product lines (polymer barriers and floor safety tape), the contact person and the legal entity details: company name, tax code and office address. Purchasing teams often need it when assessing a new vendor. For a quick look at the legal entity details, see the About Thien Khoi page.

I have the documents. What is the next step?

Print the checklist, walk the warehouse during a running shift and photograph the spots you marked. Then send the photos through the survey booking page with an estimated length and the vehicle type. Thien Khoi reviews the photos, calls you back and books a survey. To look at products first, go to the products page.
