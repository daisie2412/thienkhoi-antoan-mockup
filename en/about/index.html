Site visit first. Recommendations after.

Thien Khoi's core business is white marble from Yen Bai. This division has a different job: keeping people in factories and warehouses in Northern Vietnam's industrial parks from being hit by forklifts, crushed by racks or hurt in falls.

Four rules we keep on every survey.

Stand beside the forklift lane

Watch forklift and foot traffic during a live shift instead of guessing from drawings.

Recommend only what is needed

If new floor lines are enough, we say floor lines. If the spot needs a barrier, we say barrier.

Urgent spots first

Work in phases, so you don't have to shut down a whole area to do everything at once.

State the limits

Every product has limits, and we tell you what they are upfront. We never promise "absolute safety".

How Thien Khoi works

From the first photo to the last barrier

Thien Khoi Safety Solutions follows a fixed sequence: review photos, visit the warehouse, recommend zone by zone, install in phases. From start to finish, you deal with one contact person.

Five steps from survey to installation

Each step has one job, so you know which step the work has reached.

  • Photo review: you send photos of spots where things get hit or people slip, through the survey booking page or WhatsApp. We call back to ask about vehicle types, peak hours and the floor.
  • On-site survey: we follow the flow of goods during a running shift, measure each stretch, and check old impact marks and the floor.
  • Zone-by-zone recommendation: what to do first, what to leave for a later phase, what only needs floor lines. You choose which part to do first.
  • Phased installation: we fence off the stretch being worked on and tell that shift's drivers in advance to go around. No shutting down a whole area.
  • Handover: we show your team how to clean the barriers, check bolts and joints according to the manufacturer's maintenance guide, and how to check tape edges after some time in use.

After installation

If a forklift damages a barrier, replace only the damaged part according to the manufacturer's guide, not the whole stretch. Photograph the impact point, the base and the joints, then send them on WhatsApp so we order the right part. If one spot gets hit again and again, repair the barrier and also review the vehicle flow in that area. If you change the warehouse layout, call us to discuss moving barriers and re-marking lines.

Service area

Thien Khoi surveys and installs for factories and warehouses in Hanoi and the northern provinces. If your warehouse is farther away, send photos first. We review them, give a first suggestion, then discuss an approach that fits your location.
