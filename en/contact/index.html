Book a factory safety survey: what to send, what to prepare

Booking a factory safety survey with Thien Khoi starts with a few photos and a phone call. This section explains what photos are enough, how the survey goes and what we use your information for.

Photos to send first: not pretty, just enough

Phone photos are fine. What matters is that the photo shows where the spot is in the warehouse and what has happened to it. Send them through the form on this page or message us on WhatsApp or Zalo.

If there are several spots, number each one and put the number in the photo note, so when we call back we are talking about the same place.

  • One wide photo that shows the whole walkway, rack row or stairs
  • One or two close photos of the dent, the wear or the slippery spot
  • The estimated length of the stretch to fix, in meters
  • The vehicle type that runs there and the busiest time
  • A floor plan if you have one, not required

How the on-site survey goes

We follow the flow of goods: from the receiving door, through the racking area, to the dispatch dock, then the stairs and exit routes. In each zone, the surveyor measures the stretch to fix, takes photos, checks the floor and notes the vehicle type. Things photos do not show, such as impact marks hidden by goods or hollow concrete under the epoxy, are noted on the spot.

After the survey, you receive a recommendation split by zone: what to do first, what to do in a later phase, and what only needs a line.

When to book the visit

Book during a running shift, ideally at peak dispatch. When the warehouse is quiet, the aisles are clear and everywhere looks safe. Only when pallets spill into the lane and forklifts run nonstop do you see where people get pushed into the vehicle lane. If you run night shifts, add one visit in the evening to check poorly lit spots. If your warehouse has a cold room or production area that needs special protective gear, tell us in advance so the surveyor can prepare.

Survey area

Thien Khoi surveys in Hanoi and the northern provinces. If your warehouse is farther away, send photos first and we will review them and discuss how to proceed.

What we use your information for

The information in the form is used only to call you back, set a date and prepare a recommendation for your warehouse. We do not post photos of your warehouse on the web or social media without written consent. How we store data and how to request deletion are set out in the privacy policy.

Not sure what to photograph

Use the 10-point checklist to walk the warehouse during a shift, or read the 7 risk zones in a warehouse to know what to look for in each zone. Photos taken from the checklist can be sent as they are, with no need to retake them.

What photos are enough when booking a factory safety survey?

Phone photos are fine, they do not need to be pretty. For each spot, send a wide photo to show where it is in the warehouse and a close photo to show what has failed. Add the estimated length and the vehicle type that runs there. If you do not know what to photograph, follow the 10-point checklist first, or see the 7 zones in a warehouse.

What time should we survey to see the real problem?

During a running shift, ideally at peak dispatch. When the warehouse is quiet, every aisle looks safe. Only when vehicles run nonstop and waiting pallets spill into the lane do you see where people get pushed into the vehicle lane. If you have a night shift, add one visit in the evening. See the dock doors and dispatch area.

Which provinces does Thien Khoi survey?

Factories and warehouses in Hanoi and the northern provinces. If your warehouse is farther away, you can still book a survey by sending photos first. We review the photos, suggest a starting polymer barrier series or floor tape type for each spot, then discuss a survey approach that fits your location.

Who from our side should join the survey?

Someone who knows the vehicle flow in the warehouse, usually the warehouse manager or forklift team leader, and someone from the EHS department if you have one. The forklift team knows where hits happen and photos do not show it. EHS handles the gate pass, protective gear and the rules for walking in the production area. Read the 7 zones in a warehouse first to see where we will go.

What is the information I send used for?

To call you back, set the survey date and prepare a recommendation for your warehouse. Photos of your warehouse are posted on the web or social media only with your written consent. How we store data and how to request deletion are in the privacy policy. To see who is behind this website, see the About Thien Khoi page.
